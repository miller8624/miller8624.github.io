How you earn points

10 points per meaningful daily check-in, 20 for reaching your step goal, and 30 for your planned activity. Recovery walks count on recovery days. Points and badges come from saved cloud records; editing a date never adds duplicate points. Levels take 200 points. Rest is part of progress: missed days reset current streaks, but keep your earned points and best streak.

Steps only, home bodyweight, Apple Fitness+, gym, or active rest. Gym plans use completed gym visits: a full-body return after two clear days off, a light accessory day immediately after full body, then upper/lower emphasis on consecutive visits.

New choices count toward future gym suggestions only after you mark the activity complete and save. Older entries without a gym choice are preserved but cannot tell us which muscles you trained.

Full-body days lead with squat, bench press and a controlled deadlift pattern, plus a row. Use 2 working sets on your return, leaving 2–3 good reps in reserve; increase only as recovery permits. Log loads in Strong.

Consecutive-day suggestions are not a test of toughness. If the same muscles are still sore, choose active rest or a walk. Bodyweight pushups and squats also count as training stress; reduce or pause the challenges when recovery needs it.

Make this plan yours

Pushup targets advance by one per completed day, not per calendar day. Changing the start or initial target recalculates the challenge.

Countdown uses the confirmed date only. Your goal path is a planning guide, not a promise. Start with a four-week consistency block, then reassess; no promised trip weight. Consider a first milestone of 5% below your starting weight, revisited after four weeks.

Your data, across devices

Check-ins, reviews, and settings are stored in your private cloud database. Sign in on your phone or computer to see the same records. Saves require a connection; failed saves remain in the form for retry.

Export a backup regularly. Import your old dashboard backup once to migrate existing entries. Import merges dates into the cloud and overwrites matching dates and settings after confirmation.

No fitness records are saved to browser storage by this app. Closing or refreshing before a failed save is retried loses the unsaved form. Export is a plaintext backup: keep it private.
